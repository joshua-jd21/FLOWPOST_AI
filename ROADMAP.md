# PostPilot AI Roadmap

## Completed

- Sprint 1: Project foundation with React, Vite, TypeScript, Express, and shared tooling.
- Sprint 2: User registration, login, JWT cookie sessions, protected routes, and MongoDB integration.
- Sprint 3: Premium SaaS dashboard shell with sidebar, top navigation, profile, settings, and responsive layout.
- Sprint 4: AI Studio with Gemini caption generation, fallback handling, AI history persistence, and thumbnail support.
- Sprint 5: Connected account infrastructure for LinkedIn, Instagram, X, and Facebook.
- Sprint 6: Social scheduler with post CRUD, drafts, scheduled posts, media payloads, and publishing hooks.
- Sprint 7: Analytics and insights, campaign planner, content calendar, and AI image generation updates.
- Sprint 8: Brand Kit and persona builder for brand-aware content generation.
- Sprint 9: Competitor analysis and trend intelligence.
- Sprint 10: AI social media agent and AI Content Copilot for natural-language orchestration.
- Product polish: landing page, demo mode, onboarding, documentation, deployment preparation, and production-readiness pass.
